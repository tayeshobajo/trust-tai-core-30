# Daily workspace: guided scenario and usability scorecard

Round 9 of the agency journey build. This document is the script and the score
sheet for a real usability run with the team. It is not evidence. Nothing here
may be marked passed by anyone except the people who run it.

## What the workspace promises

- Home is the personal entry. Three lists, in the same order, every day:
  **My next actions**, **Prepared for you**, **Decisions needed**.
- Each row says what it is, why it is there, whose it is, and one thing to do.
- The verbs never change: Open, Review, Edit, Approve, Assign, Done.
- Preparation detail is collapsed until someone asks for it.
- The client workspace carries the continuity; Conductor is optional and can be
  ignored entirely without losing access to any work.

## Five minute guided scenario

Run with one teammate at a time. Do not coach, do not point, do not narrate.
Read the italics aloud and then stop talking.

1. *You have just sat down. Find the thing you should do first today.*
   Start a timer at the end of the sentence. Stop it when they say out loud
   which item they have chosen. Record the seconds.
2. *Tell me why that one.* Record whether the reason they give is the reason the
   row gives.
3. *Do the next step on it.* Record whether they reached the right room without
   asking where it lives.
4. *Somebody else should pick this up from here. Hand it over.* Record whether
   the handoff completed and whether anything had to be retyped.
5. *Is there anything waiting on your decision?* Record whether they found the
   Decisions needed list unaided.
6. *Was any of that number on the screen surprising or unclear?* Record verbatim.

Repeat at 1440, 768 and 375 pixels wide, and once using the keyboard only.

## Scorecard

One sheet per teammate. Keep every sheet, including failures.

| # | Measure | Record | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U1 | Seconds to name the next task | seconds | 30 or under |
| U2 | Their reason matches the row's reason | yes / no | yes |
| U3 | Reached the right room unaided | yes / no | yes |
| U4 | Core handoff completed without coaching | yes / no | yes |
| U5 | Nothing had to be retyped in the handoff | yes / no | yes |
| U6 | Found Decisions needed unaided | yes / no | yes |
| U7 | Keyboard only: reached and acted on a row | yes / no | yes |
| U8 | 375 and 768 wide: found the same row | yes / no | yes |
| U9 | No number on screen was unclear or unexplained | yes / no | yes |
| U10 | Words they used that the screen did not use | verbatim | recorded |

**Overall target:** at least 4 of 5 representative teammates pass U1 and U4.

## Who may record a result

Only the person running the session, naming the teammate's role and the date.
Preparation cannot award itself a usability pass, and a code test is not a
substitute for a person sitting down in front of the screen.
