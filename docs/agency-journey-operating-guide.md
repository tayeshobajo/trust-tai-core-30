# Trust Tai operating guide: the agency journey

Round 10 of the agency journey build. This is the guide the team would use, the
rollout by role, the list of what is switched on and off, and what to do when
something goes wrong. Nothing in this guide is deployed, enabled or scheduled.

## The journey in plain words

1. **An enquiry arrives.** It keeps where it came from, when, and what they
   actually wrote. A repeat of the same enquiry is recognised as a repeat.
2. **Somebody qualifies it.** A packet separates what we know from what we are
   guessing, names what we do not know, and judges fit against a written ICP.
   Only a named person qualifies, passes or defers.
3. **A conversation opens.** Exactly one, for a qualified enquiry, with an owner.
4. **Discovery is recorded.** The outcome they want, their constraints, who is
   involved, how they will judge success, and where each line came from. The raw
   notes stay exactly as taken.
5. **A roadmap is prepared.** Point A to point B, in phases, with options,
   dependencies, one first move and the unknowns named. A named person approves
   the destination and the order; approval freezes that version.
6. **A proposal is written from one frozen version.** Scope, exclusions,
   deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, responsibilities, pricing and
   next steps, each line pointing back at its phase.
7. **The agreement and the money are read separately.** Proposed, approved,
   sent, accepted or declined; not invoiced, invoiced or paid. Only a person
   moves either past the proposal stage.
8. **Onboarding runs the same checklist every time**, and opens exactly one
   project workspace when everything is clear.
9. **Delivery carries its own proof.** Work, acceptance and the client outcome
   stay three separate readings.
10. **Care, review and what comes next.** Health is read, never assumed; results
    are compared against the baseline and target; a next roadmap opens once.

## Rollout by role

| Role | What they do on day one | What they never do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Approves destinations, prices, dates and any exception; enables or leaves disabled each preparation job | Nothing is barred, but nothing is automatic either |
| Admin | Same approvals as owner where policy records it; manages access references | Cannot widen anyone's permissions by asking |
| Account lead | Qualifies enquiries, owns conversations, approves discovery, raises renewals | Cannot approve price, date or scope alone unless policy already says so |
| Delivery lead | Accepts task owners and dates against recorded availability, decides milestone acceptance | Cannot commit a date with no recorded availability |
| Team member | Opens, reviews, edits and completes their own work; hands over with sources intact | Cannot approve, send or change a commitment |

Everyone starts on Home. Nobody has to visit every room.

## Automation inventory

| Job | State today | Turned on by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification packet for a new enquiry | Disabled in every workspace | An authorised user, explicitly |
| Summary and suggested actions for a new conversation | Disabled in every workspace | An authorised user, explicitly |
| Status draft on a milestone change | Disabled in every workspace | An authorised user, explicitly |

Every job prepares only. None of them sends, publishes, pays, or changes a
commitment. There is no send path in any of them.

## Monitoring, stopping and recovering

- **Watch:** how many preparations ran, how many finished, how many said they
  could not finish and why. A job that cannot finish says so; it does not write
  a half result.
- **Stop:** each job has an off switch, per workspace, and a workspace-wide stop.
  Turning a job off stops future runs; it deletes nothing already prepared.
- **Recover:** a repeated event prepares the same thing once. A retry after a
  failed save writes one record, not two. An uncertain outcome is left as a
  state to reconcile, never retried automatically.
- **Roll back:** the way back is to turn the job off. Prepared output stays
  readable, decisions already made by people stay exactly as they were, frozen
  roadmap versions stay frozen, and no safeguard is removed to undo anything.
  There is no rollback that deletes a person's decision or a recorded approval.

## What this guide is not

It is not a deployment plan, and nothing here has been run with real clients.
Rollout is reviewed separately before anything is switched on.
